# hardroxide-psu

Zynq UltraScale+ processing-system support for bare metal on the Cortex-A53 (AArch64, EL3, one core): startup and exception vectors, MMU
and caches, the GIC, the generic timer, clocks, the Cadence UART and a console. The counterpart of `hardroxide-ps7` for the Zynq-7000.
Part of [Hardroxide](https://github.com/jwkunz/Hardroxide); the book is at <https://jwkunz.github.io/Hardroxide/>.

`no_std`, no third-party dependencies. Licensed under either of Apache-2.0 or MIT at your option.
