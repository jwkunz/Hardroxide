# hardroxide-ps7

Zynq-7000 processing-system support for bare metal: startup and exception vectors for
the Cortex-A9, the GIC, the global and private timers, the Cadence UART and a console.
Part of [Hardroxide](https://github.com/jwkunz/Hardroxide); the book is at
<https://jwkunz.github.io/Hardroxide/>.

`no_std`, no third-party dependencies. Licensed under either of Apache-2.0 or MIT at your
option.
